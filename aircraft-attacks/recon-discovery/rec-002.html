BS-REC-002: Avionics Architecture & Protocol Mapping

Identifying specific avionics hardware, software versions, and data bus protocols used by the target aircraft.

Technique Description

🌐 Remote

The adversary seeks to determine the specific Flight Management Computer (FMC/FMS), Electronic Flight Bag (EFB) software, ACARS hardware, and data buses utilized by the aircraft. The primary goal is to map the communication protocols and data pathways. This intelligence is gathered by analyzing manufacturer documentation, technical forums, maintenance manuals, and open-source aviation research to understand how data flows within the target's avionics suite.

Targeted Systems: FMS, EFB, ACARS, ARINC 429/717, AFDX networks.
