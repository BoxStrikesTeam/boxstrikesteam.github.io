BS-DIS-001: Cabin Network & Port Enumeration

Enumerating in-flight entertainment (IFE) networks, Wi-Fi SSIDs, and exposed services post-initial access.

Technique Description

🟡 Local Network

Once initial access is gained (e.g., via a passenger device or compromised cabin Wi-Fi), the adversary enumerates the local network. This involves discovering the Cabin Wi-Fi SSID, testing for weak encryption, and scanning for misconfigured open ports. If the aircraft's systems are exposed to external networks, attackers may also use platforms like Shodan to pre-enumerate these ports and gather basic topology information to plan lateral movement.

Targeted Systems: IFE Systems, In-flight Wi-Fi Routers, Cabin Domain Controllers.
