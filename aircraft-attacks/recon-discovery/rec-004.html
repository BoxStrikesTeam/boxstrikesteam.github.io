BS-REC-004: Personnel & Social Engineering Targeting

Identifying and profiling aviation staff to prepare for social engineering or insider threat recruitment.

Technique Description

🌐 Remote

If historical aircraft data or operator information is insufficient, the adversary targets human assets to gather dynamic or real-time intelligence. Personnel such as pilots, maintenance technicians, and IT staff are identified via LinkedIn, press releases, or data leaks. This profiling is conducted to prepare highly targeted social engineering campaigns (e.g., spear-phishing) or to recruit insiders for physical access.

Targeted Systems: Human Intelligence (HUMINT), Corporate Directories, Social Media.
