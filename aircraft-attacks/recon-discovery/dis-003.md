            <h1 class="section-title" style="margin-top:15px;">BS-DIS-003: GPS/GNSS Signal Structure & Data Decoding</h1>
            <p class="section-subtitle">Intercepting and analyzing raw 1575.42 MHz L1 signals to reverse-engineer the target's navigation data format prior to manipulation.</p>
            
            <div class="card" style="margin-top: 30px; border-left: 4px solid #7c3aed;">
                <div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: start; flex-wrap: wrap;">
                    <h3>Technique Description</h3>
                    <span style="background: #fce7f3; color: #9d174d; font-size: 0.8rem; font-weight: 600; padding: 4px 10px; border-radius: 4px;">📡 RF / Physical</span>
                </div>
                <p style="margin-top: 15px; color: var(--text);">
                    Before an adversary can manipulate (spoof) an aircraft's GPS systems, they must first understand the target's specific data structure and message types. Using hardware antennas and Software Defined Radio (SDR), the attacker captures the 1575.42 MHz (L1 frequency) signal emitted or received by the target. The raw signal is visualized and recorded. Subsequently, specialized software is used to process the signal, extract satellite data, and decode the exact message format and navigation information. By reverse-engineering the signal structure from the raw data, the adversary gains the necessary blueprint to craft valid, undetectable spoofed signals in the subsequent attack phases.
                </p>
                <div style="margin-top: 20px; padding-top: 15px; border-top: 1px solid var(--border);">
                    <strong style="color: var(--primary);">Targeted Systems:</strong>
                    <span style="color: var(--text-light);"> GPS/GNSS Receivers (L1/L2/L5 bands), SDR Hardware, Navigation Databases, Flight Management Systems (FMS).</span>
                </div>
                <div style="margin-top: 15px; padding-top: 15px; border-top: 1px solid var(--border);">
                    <strong style="color: var(--primary);">Box Strike Note:</strong>
                    <span style="color: var(--text-light);"> This is a critical precursor to <em>GNSS Spoofing</em>. Without decoding the exact NMEA or proprietary binary message structures used by the specific avionics GPS receiver (e.g., Honeywell, Collins), a spoofed signal will be rejected by the aircraft's internal consistency checks (like RAIM).</span>
                </div>
