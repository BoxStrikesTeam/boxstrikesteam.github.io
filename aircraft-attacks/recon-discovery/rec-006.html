BS-REC-006: Supply Chain & Vendor Ecosystem Research

Mapping the third-party software and hardware providers to identify weak links in the aviation supply chain.

Technique Description

🌐 Remote

Adversaries actively research the software and hardware vendors integrated into the target airline's ecosystem using open-source intelligence (OSINT). This reconnaissance primarily focuses on two critical groups: 1) Avionics hardware and software suppliers (e.g., OEMs, FMS developers) and 2) Passenger Service Systems (PSS) and corporate IT vendors (e.g., reservation systems, check-in kiosks, cloud providers). By mapping the supply chain, attackers identify potential weak links, shared dependencies, and third-party vendors that may have lower cybersecurity maturity than the airline itself.

Targeted Systems: Avionics OEMs, PSS Providers (e.g., SITA, Amadeus), MRO Software Vendors, Cloud/IT Infrastructure Partners.
Box Strike Note: This phase is a direct precursor to Supply Chain Compromise (Initial Access). If a vendor is breached, the adversary can push malicious updates or backdoors directly to the aircraft or airport infrastructure.
