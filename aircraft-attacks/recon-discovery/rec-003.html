BS-REC-003: Operator & Supply Chain Analysis

Assessing the cybersecurity maturity, maintenance procedures, and third-party vendors of the airline or airport.

Technique Description

🌐 Remote

Adversaries investigate the airline operator's or airport's cybersecurity maturity, maintenance procedures, and supply chain vendors. By analyzing open-source documents, public procurement/tender announcements, and corporate policies, the attacker evaluates how strictly the target adheres to cybersecurity protocols (e.g., DO-326A) and identifies weak links in the third-party maintenance or software supply chain.

Targeted Systems: MRO facilities, Airline IT/OT infrastructure, Vendor portals.
